Scavengia

Games

Scavengia is an AI-powered index of everything, and people build it by exploring the world. Players go out, open caches, and pay to explore. Each discovery adds real places, objects, images, lore, and rarity to a shared map of the planet. When everything has been found, the AI keeps digging. It surfaces the precedent: the history and the earlier finds behind each place. It connects people on opposite sides of the world through the same hunt. Ride-alongs fix two problems at once. Someone who is driving and can't play takes along someone who can play but can't move. The driver moves the game forward and the remote player gets to explore. Nobody is left out of the hunt.

  • TypeScript
  • Capacitor Android

RecordOfLaw

Civic/Politics

Who made the law, when, and how. RecordOfLaw records offices, officeholders, and legal changes. Every entry is dated, sourced, and cross-linked. It starts with FDR and the New Deal, and the build checks its data before it compiles. The 2026 Midterm Tracker is live.
