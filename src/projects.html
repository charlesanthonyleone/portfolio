Scavengia

Games

Scavengia is an AI-powered index of everything, and people build it by exploring the world. Players go out, open caches, and pay to explore. Each discovery adds real places, objects, images, lore, and rarity to a shared map of the planet. When everything has been found, the AI keeps digging. It surfaces the precedent: the history and the earlier finds behind each place. It connects people on opposite sides of the world through the same hunt. Ride-alongs fix two problems at once. Someone who is driving and can't play takes along someone who can play but can't move. The driver moves the game forward and the remote player gets to explore. Nobody is left out of the hunt.

  • TypeScript
  • Capacitor Android

XatCast

Community

A broadcast network that airs only work the public is free to use. It runs always-on stations (XatArt, XatWar, XatSports, XatMusic) fed from the Met, the Art Institute of Chicago, Wikimedia Commons, and the Internet Archive. The license and attribution are recorded for every item. A shared broadcast clock means every viewer sees the same thing at the same second.

XatMaps · WarMap

Civic/Politics

A live 3D night globe of world conflict. Free, open source, with no accounts, no paywall, and no tracking. It combines UCDP, GDELT (labeled "auto-coded, unverified"), NASA FIRMS heat detections, and Wikipedia control maps. Every event shows its source and a confidence label. It also has a daily brief, RSS feeds, and an embed mode.
