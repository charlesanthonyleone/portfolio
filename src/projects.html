XatCast

Community

Always-on TV stations (XatArt, XatWar, XatSports, XatMusic) airing only public-domain and openly licensed works from the Met, the Art Institute of Chicago, Wikimedia Commons and the Internet Archive, with license and attribution recorded per item. A deterministic broadcast clock means every viewer sees the same item at the same second.

XatMaps · WarMap

Civic/Politics

Free, open-source live conflict maps — no accounts, no paywall, no tracking. A 3D night globe of UCDP GED events, GDELT events (labeled “auto-coded, unverified”), NASA FIRMS heat detections and attributed Wikipedia control maps, with a daily brief, RSS and embed mode. Every event shows its source and a confidence label.
